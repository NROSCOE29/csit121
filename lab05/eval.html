Who is the sites target audience?

Fans of kingdom hearts or people looking for information on any aspect of the main 3 games.

How is the site orginized

The site uses hierarchical orginization

Which CRAP Design Principle does the site use? Provide at least one example.

This site uses the repitition design principle. You see the same color palettes and fonts within the website. Even more so with it being a guide site for a video game you see repeating charecters and worlds as well when viewing the site.

What is the Audit Score according to the Accessibility Checker?

The site got a 48 out of 100 on the audit score.

What is the site's effectiveness? Does it help users complete actions accurately?

It is very effective if you are looking for information on the first 3 kingdom hearts games. It gives walkthroughs on the main story for the main 3 games as well as breakdowns on how to 100 percent the games. Along with other things to unlock and discover in the games.
